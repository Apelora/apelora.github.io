Questions about licensing and services

Do I need to buy anything to use Apelora Fabric?

No. The whole software is available under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3. Anyone can use it, including companies, as long as they follow the licence.

Can a business use the Community edition?

Yes. Being a business does not require anything extra. The AGPL asks that if you modify the software and let other people use your modified version over a network, you offer them its source. Read the licence summary and the full licence text, and ask a lawyer if you are unsure how it applies to you.

Do paid services change what the software can do?

No. Services add our time: installation, support, training, integration. There is one edition of the software, and no feature is held back for customers.

Why are there no prices?

Every environment is different, and the services have not been priced yet. We quote after a short conversation about your servers, network, storage and how you operate. Until then the answer is "request a quote", not a guess.

Is Apelora Fabric ready for production?

Not yet. It is in early development and has been tested on a four-server lab, not on real servers. We will discuss your use, and what the software is ready for, before recommending anything. See the roadmap.

Tell us about your environment

How many servers, what storage and network, who needs access. We will tell you what Apelora does for that today and what it does not.
