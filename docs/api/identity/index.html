    Identity and access API

    Identity and access: organisations, projects, people, groups, roles and assignments, approvals, service identities, tokens and sign-in methods. This page lists 66 operations.

    Set variables in the API overview. Download OpenAPI.

    Operations

    OperationWhat it does
    GET /api/v1/approvalsRequests and approvals: your own, plus every open one if you may approve.
    POST /api/v1/approvalsAsks for approval of one risky action on one target.
    POST /api/v1/approvals/{id}/approveApproves a request made by someone else.
    POST /api/v1/approvals/{id}/cancelWithdraws your own pending request.
    POST /api/v1/approvals/{id}/rejectRejects a request.
    GET /api/v1/identity/assignmentsRole assignments in the caller's scopes.
    POST /api/v1/identity/assignmentsGrants a role to a person, group or automation identity at a scope.
    DELETE /api/v1/identity/assignments/{assignment}Revokes an assignment; the person loses it on their next request.
    POST /api/v1/identity/assignments/previewShows what an assignment would give before it is saved: what the person can already do at the scope, what they could do afterwards, which permissions would be inert, and whether you may save it.
    GET /api/v1/identity/auditAudit history the caller may read: the same filters and paging as GET /api/v1/audit.
    POST /api/v1/identity/emergency-accessGrants a person a platform role for a short time, with a recorded reason (break-glass).
    POST /api/v1/identity/environments/{id}/incus-projectCreates or adopts the environment's Incus project now (idempotent): the restricted project with its default profile, granted to the deploy identity, then the quotas mirrored.
    GET /api/v1/identity/groupsGroups the caller may see.
    POST /api/v1/identity/groupsCreates a group: an organisation's (needs group.manage there) or a platform group (platform group.manage).
    PUT /api/v1/identity/groups/{group}/externalMaps a platform group onto a group of an identity provider or directory, or sets the source of a mapping made before sources were recorded (needsSource).
    GET /api/v1/identity/groups/{group}/membersMembers of a group.
    PUT /api/v1/identity/groups/{group}/members/{user}Adds a person to a group.
    DELETE /api/v1/identity/groups/{group}/members/{user}Removes a person from a group; their access through it ends on their next request.
    POST /api/v1/identity/local-accountsCreates a local account and returns its one-time setup link, valid for 24 hours.
    POST /api/v1/identity/local-accounts/{id}/resetTakes a local account's password away, revokes every session of theirs and returns a new one-time setup link (24 hours).
    GET /api/v1/identity/organizationsOrganisations the caller may see.
    POST /api/v1/identity/organizationsCreates an organisation.
    POST /api/v1/identity/organizations/{org}/projectsCreates a project in an organisation.
    GET /api/v1/identity/permissionsEvery permission, its class and the narrowest scope at which a grant takes effect.
    GET /api/v1/identity/personal-tokensEveryone's personal tokens (or one person's), live ones and those that ended in the last 30 days.
    DELETE /api/v1/identity/personal-tokens/{id}Revokes anyone's live personal token.
    GET /api/v1/identity/policiesAccess policies (MFA and approval rules).
    PUT /api/v1/identity/policiesCreates or updates a policy by name.
    DELETE /api/v1/identity/policies/{id}Deletes a policy.
    GET /api/v1/identity/projectsProjects the caller may see, optionally of one organisation.
    GET /api/v1/identity/projects/{project}/environmentsEnvironments of a project.
    POST /api/v1/identity/projects/{project}/environmentsCreates an environment.
    GET /api/v1/identity/quotas/{scope}/{id}A scope's quota and use.
    PUT /api/v1/identity/quotas/{scope}/{id}Sets a scope's quota (all limits empty removes it), then mirrors the tightest applicable quota onto every environment beneath it.
    GET /api/v1/identity/rolesThe predefined and custom roles with their permissions.
    POST /api/v1/identity/rolesDefines a custom role from explicit permissions.
    DELETE /api/v1/identity/roles/{role}Deletes a custom role that no assignment uses.
    GET /api/v1/identity/service-identitiesRegistered service identities (the credentials Apelora uses for Incus and MAAS, and automation identities that call this API).
    POST /api/v1/identity/service-identitiesRegisters a service identity.
    POST /api/v1/identity/service-identities/{id}/disableDisables a service identity at once.
    POST /api/v1/identity/service-identities/{id}/enableRe-enables a service identity.
    GET /api/v1/identity/sign-inSign-in settings: identity providers (saved and from the environment), whether API tokens are accepted, and local accounts.
    PUT /api/v1/identity/sign-in/api-tokensTurns API tokens on or off.
    POST /api/v1/identity/sign-in/checkReads a provider's discovery document and key set, without saving anything: what an administrator checks before saving.
    GET /api/v1/identity/sign-in/directoriesThe directories people sign in with, each checked with a TLS connection now.
    POST /api/v1/identity/sign-in/directoriesAdds a directory; it is offered on the sign-in page at once when enabled.
    PUT /api/v1/identity/sign-in/directories/{id}Changes a directory (including turning it off).
    DELETE /api/v1/identity/sign-in/directories/{id}Removes a directory: its people can no longer sign in with it; their accounts and roles in Apelora stay.
    POST /api/v1/identity/sign-in/directories/checkConnects with TLS, binds as a test person and reads their entry and groups, without saving anything.
    PUT /api/v1/identity/sign-in/local-accountsTurns sign-in with local accounts on or off.
    POST /api/v1/identity/sign-in/providersAdds an identity provider; it is checked and applied at once.
    PUT /api/v1/identity/sign-in/providers/{id}Changes a provider saved in the Console.
    DELETE /api/v1/identity/sign-in/providers/{id}Removes a provider saved in the Console: people stop being able to sign in with it.
    GET /api/v1/identity/sign-insEveryone's Apelora sessions open now (or one person's).
    DELETE /api/v1/identity/sign-ins/{id}Ends anyone's session.
    GET /api/v1/identity/tenancyOrganisations, projects and environments the caller can see, with quotas, use and each environment's Incus project as Incus reports it.
    GET /api/v1/identity/usersPeople who have signed in, as far as the caller may see them: everyone for a platform administrator, otherwise those with a role in the caller's scopes.
    POST /api/v1/identity/users/{user}/disableDisables a person: their tokens and Apelora sessions stop working at once.
    POST /api/v1/identity/users/{user}/enableRe-enables a person.
    GET /api/v1/identity/users/lookupFinds one person by exact user name or e-mail so a role can be given to them.
    GET /api/v1/me/sign-insYour Apelora sessions open now (password and directory sign-ins), most recently used first.
    DELETE /api/v1/me/sign-ins/{id}Ends one of your sessions (signs that browser out).
    GET /api/v1/me/tokensYour personal tokens (live ones, and those that ended in the last 30 days), newest first.
    POST /api/v1/me/tokensCreates a personal token, shown only in this response.
    DELETE /api/v1/me/tokens/{id}Revokes one of your live tokens; it stops working at once.
    POST /api/v1/me/tokens/{id}/rotateRotates one of your live tokens: a successor with the same name (shown only in this response), and the old token keeps working for the overlap (0–1440 minutes, 60 by default), then expires.
