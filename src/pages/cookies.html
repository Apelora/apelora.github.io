What we use

None. The site sets no cookies and stores nothing else in your browser. It uses no tracking pixels and does not try to recognise your device. You can check this in your browser's developer tools, under storage and network. Everything loads from this site's own address.

Why there is no cookie banner

A banner asks permission for cookies and similar tools that are not strictly needed. This site has none, so there is nothing to agree to or refuse, and a banner would only be one more thing in your way. The pages also tell your browser to load nothing from another site, so one cannot be added by accident.

GitHub

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, and some pages link to GitHub. GitHub has its own cookie rules for its own pages, described in its cookie notice. They apply once you leave this site.

If this changes

Before we add anything that is not strictly needed, such as analytics, we will update this page and the privacy policy first. Then the site will ask you, with Accept and Reject looking the same and equally easy. Nothing will load before you choose, and a link in the footer will let you change your mind.
