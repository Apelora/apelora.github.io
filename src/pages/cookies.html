Cookie policy

This website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

Last updated 25 October 2026. All legal pages.

What we use

None. The site does not set cookies, and it does not use local storage, session storage, IndexedDB, device fingerprinting or tracking pixels. You can check it yourself: open your browser's developer tools, and look at the storage and network panels while you browse. Everything loads from this site's own address.

Why there is no cookie banner

A banner asks permission for cookies and similar tools that are not strictly needed. This site has none, so there is nothing to agree to or refuse, and a banner would only be one more thing in your way. The pages also tell your browser to load nothing from another site, so one cannot be added by accident.

GitHub

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, and some pages link to GitHub. GitHub has its own cookie rules for its own pages, described in its cookie notice. They apply once you leave this site.

If this changes

Before we add anything that is not strictly necessary, such as analytics, we will first update this page and the privacy policy, and show a choice on the site where Accept and Reject look the same and are equally easy, nothing loads before you choose, and you can change your mind later from a link in the footer.
