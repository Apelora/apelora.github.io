Application developer

Designs an application and deploys it.

Application administrator

Keeps a running fabric healthy.

  • Fabric Console. Servers, health, alerts, backups, roles, tokens and the audit log.
  • A failed server. What restarts on its own, what waits, and how a person confirms a server is off.
  • Access. Who may deploy, who may change the cluster, and what is written down afterwards.

How you earn one

The same three steps for every track. Length and format are agreed in the quote. The software itself stays free.

  1. The course

    Taught on a lab cluster, using Studio or the Fabric Console for that track. The architect course uses both.

  2. The assessment

    A practical task: deploy a small application, recover from a failed server, or review a design and name what is not built yet.

  3. The certificate

    It names you, the track and the date. It is not a licence to operate someone else’s cluster, and it does not expire on a timer we have not published.

What the certificate means

It records that you completed that course and that assessment. It does not say the product is ready for production on your servers.

It says

  • Which of the three tracks you completed
  • The date of the assessment
  • That the work was done on a lab, with the current software

It does not say

  • That a public exam is open, or that a price is published
  • That an outside body accredited the certificate
  • That every module, or a physical server, was part of the assessment
