Why not Proxmox VE or VMware?

If looking after virtual machines yourself is what you want, both are mature choices. VMware is closed source and paid per CPU core. Apelora Fabric is open source, for teams that would rather describe the application and let the platform do the rest.

  • You draw the application, not the machines: web servers, a database, a cache, and which connects to which.
  • Its network follows the drawing: the application gets its own network, and only the connections you drew are allowed.
  • Replicas sit behind one address with health checks, on different servers. A lost replica is replaced, and before a server goes into maintenance its replicas stop receiving requests.
  • Risky actions need a second person, and every change is recorded with who made it.

Apelora Fabric runs on Incus, the open source system container and virtual machine manager. It is in early development; see what works today.

When to choose another

Choose Proxmox VE when…

You want to look after virtual machines and containers yourself, one by one, with a long track record.

Choose VMware when…

You want an established platform for virtual machines with vendor support, and per-core pricing fits your budget.

Choose Kubernetes when…

Your team builds containerised services, knows the tools, and can add what it leaves to you, such as an outside load balancer.

Choose Apelora Fabric when…

You want to run applications, not machines, on your own Linux servers: draw it once and the network, failover and approvals are handled. It is early software, so start with a lab and talk to us.
