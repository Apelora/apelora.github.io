Deployment

We install Apelora Fabric on your servers, prove that it works there and hand it over.

  1. Review

    Servers, network, storage, identity provider and how you operate today.

  2. Install

    Control plane, worker and Console, connected to your identity provider and secured with TLS; servers joined with single-use tokens.

  3. Validate

    Preflight on every server, test deployments, and a failure test you can watch.

  4. Hand over

    Roles, backups, alerts and runbooks configured and explained; the audit log on from the first change.

You receive

  • A working cluster with Apelora Fabric installed and connected to your identity provider
  • A written record of what was installed and how it was validated
  • Runbooks adapted to your environment, and a handover session

Outside the standard scope

  • Buying or racking hardware
  • Installing operating systems on servers
  • Migrating existing applications

We can quote these as custom services.

Integration

Connect Apelora Fabric to what you already run.

  • Identity

    OpenID Connect providers and LDAP directories, with roles mapped to your groups.

  • Monitoring

    The metrics endpoint and signed alert webhooks into your own monitoring and paging.

  • Provisioning

    Discovery of servers from MAAS, if you use it.

  • Image mirrors

    Mirrors and signed bundles for sites without internet access.

  • Custom modules

    Your own module definitions, ports and defaults in the catalog.

  • Larger deployments

    More servers, more sites, and the questions that come with them.

Training

For the people who will design with Fabric and the people who will keep it running. Format and length are agreed in the quote.

  • For designers

    Studio: modules, ports and connections, plans, deploying and changing an application.

  • For operators

    Fabric Console: servers, maintenance, health, alerts, backups and restore, roles and the audit log.

  • For incidents

    The runbooks, walked through on your own cluster: a server fails, quorum is lost, a certificate expires.

Support

A subscription with defined coverage. Proposed scope below; the final terms, hours, channels and response times are written in each quote.

Covered

  • Questions about installing, configuring and operating Apelora Fabric
  • Diagnosing problems from your logs, the support bundle and the audit log
  • Guidance for upgrades of Apelora Fabric and of the Incus release it is pinned to
  • Fixes for confirmed defects in Apelora Fabric, delivered in releases
  • Help with failed servers, using the runbooks

Not covered

  • The code and images of your own applications
  • Hardware, network and storage faults outside Apelora Fabric
  • Incus, OVN, PostgreSQL and other upstream software beyond how Fabric configures them; defects there are reported upstream
  • Features that are on the roadmap but not built
